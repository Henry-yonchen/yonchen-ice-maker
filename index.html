Who We Are

Global Ice Machine Manufacturer Rooted in China's Smart Manufacturing Hub

YONCHEN is strategically located in Cixi, Ningbo — the world-renowned home appliance intelligent manufacturing industrial belt. We specialize in the R&D, customization, and global market distribution of commercial ice machines, home ice machines, and kitchen refrigeration equipment.

Our products serve six major global markets: Europe, the Americas, the Middle East, Africa, Southeast Asia, and Australia. We partner with import distributors, restaurant chains, and engineering procurement clients worldwide.

Our core R&D and technical team brings over 20 years of deep expertise in refrigeration technology. We are proficient in global voltage standards, climate adaptability, and country-specific certification requirements. Every machine is optimized for its target environment — from high-temperature tropical zones to sub-zero cold regions.

All products carry CE, ETL, and ISO certifications. We offer flexible OEM branding, specification customization, and SKD (Semi-Knocked-Down) assembly options to match your market needs.

FAQ

Ice Machine Buying Questions, Answered

Answers to the questions importers and distributors ask us most before placing an order.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the model and whether it is a stock or OEM order. Standard models start around 100–300 units per model; OEM/ODM orders can start lower depending on the specification. Trial samples are available before bulk orders.

Can I customize the ice machines with my own brand (OEM/ODM)?

Yes. We offer full OEM/ODM services including your logo, panel color, control interface, packaging and manual. We also adapt voltage, plug type and refrigerant to your target market's requirements.

What certifications do your ice machines have?

Our products are CE, ETL and ISO 9001 certified. We can also arrange market-specific certifications such as SASO, SAA, SABS, KC and PSE for your destination country.

How long is the production and delivery lead time?

Samples ship within 7–10 days. Bulk production typically takes 25–35 days depending on order size and customization, plus shipping time from Ningbo or Shanghai port.

What voltage and plug standards do you support?

We manufacture for 110–120V and 220–240V markets with US, EU, UK, AU and other plug types. We configure the correct voltage, frequency and plug for your destination country.

Do you offer after-sales support and spare parts?

Yes. Every machine includes a warranty, and we supply spare parts, installation guidance and remote technical support. We can also provide SKD assembly kits for local assembly.

What are your shipping terms and which port do you use?

We ship FOB Ningbo or Shanghai, and also support CIF, DDP and EXW terms. We work with reliable forwarders to deliver to 60+ countries worldwide.

What snowflake ice is for

Snowflake ice is fine, soft and quick-melting. It is not what you want in a whisky glass; it is what you want in desserts, shaved-ice drinks, smoothies and cold displays where the ice is part of the texture rather than a coolant. That distinction decides whether the model sells in your market at all.

Twenty-five kilograms a day is large for a home machine — roughly 100–120 dessert or drink servings. The one-minute first ice matters more in practice: snowflake machines are often switched on for a specific moment, and a machine that takes twenty minutes to start is a machine that gets used once.

Where it fits

365 × 235 × 265 mm, 150 W, manual fill. Low noise and the child lock are what make it saleable to households rather than only to small food businesses — both are things retail buyers ask about explicitly.

Typical buyers: home dessert and smoothie users, small cafés adding a shaved-ice item, and distributors selling into Asian food and beverage channels where snowflake ice is an established product.

About the AIM-008

Is snowflake ice the same as shaved ice?

Not exactly. This machine produces fine snowflake ice continuously; shaved ice is normally made by shaving a block. For desserts the result is similar, but this machine does not need a block to start from.

What is the bin capacity?

Bin capacity varies by configuration on this model and is confirmed with the quotation rather than assumed from the catalogue. If bin size matters to your market, ask for it in writing before ordering.

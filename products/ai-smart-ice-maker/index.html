What this series is for

The AIM series is the product line for buyers selling into consumer channels: e-commerce listings, appliance retail, gift and home categories. Every model carries a control interface that can be localised — touch panel, colour display or AI voice module — and the outer glass panel can be customised with your brand artwork.

Ten models cover five ice shapes in 12–60 kg/24h capacity, which lets a single container mix several SKUs without splitting the order across suppliers.

Typical applications

  • Home kitchen and office
  • E-commerce and appliance retail
  • Hotel rooms and apartments
  • Gift and promotional programmes
All 10 models in this series can be private labelled: logo, panel colour, control interface, packaging, voltage, plug type and refrigerant are configured for your market. Samples ship in 7–10 days; bulk production 25–35 days.

Buying Questions

AI Smart Ice Makers — questions buyers ask

Can the control panel and glass artwork be customised?

Yes. The front glass panel, logo, control wording and packaging artwork are all part of the OEM scope. Voice prompt language can be changed for the destination market.

Which ice shapes are available in this series?

Bullet, square cube, crescent, spherical and snowflake ice, depending on model. Ice shape is fixed per model, so pick the shape your market sells best and build the SKU range around it.

Is this series suitable for commercial use?

The 60 kg/24h models can serve small cafés and offices. For continuous commercial duty above that, use the SIM or CIM series instead.

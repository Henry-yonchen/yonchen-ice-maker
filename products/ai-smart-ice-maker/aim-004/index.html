Why three functions in one 15 kg machine

The daily output is deliberately modest: 15 kg of cubes is roughly 60-70 drinks, which is what a counter or a break room consumes. What changes the buying decision is the third function — slush and chilled water come from the same cabinet, so a venue that sells granita or iced water alongside soft drinks does not need two purchases.

The 1 kg bin tells you the operating pattern: this is a dispense-to-order machine. It fills a glass rather than stocking a service station, which is why it is specified for front-of-house counters and hospitality rooms rather than kitchens.

Where it fits

460 × 300 × 470 mm and 120 W — countertop, standard socket, manual fill. The large touchscreen is the control surface, and the AI voice module is optional rather than standard, so the same cabinet can be sold at two price points in one market.

Typical buyers: hotel rooms and suites, office pantries, small beverage counters, showrooms, and distributors building a premium appliance line where a plain ice maker would not differentiate.

About the AIM-004

Does it make all three at once?

One function at a time, selected on the touchscreen. The value is that a single unit covers three products a venue would otherwise buy separately, not that it produces them simultaneously.

Is the AI voice module included?

It is optional. The same model is supplied with or without it, which lets you position two price points without stocking two different cabinets. Tell us the split you want at order stage.

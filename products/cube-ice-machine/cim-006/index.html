CIM-006 Cube Ice Maker — 80 kg/24h

A split-type cube machine built for continuous service in a small kitchen — 80 kg of cube ice per day with a 15 kg bin, in a footprint that fits a back-of-house corner rather than a countertop.

What 80 kg/24h actually covers

Eighty kilograms a day is roughly 350–400 drinks served over ice, or 60–80 restaurant covers across two services with ice left over for chilling. The estimate assumes 0.2–0.25 kg per drink and 0.5–1 kg per cover — treat it as a planning figure and check it against your own service data.

The important number next to output is the 15 kg bin. That is the buffer you draw on between production cycles, so a busy hour does not stall on ice that has not been made yet. If your peak is longer than the bin lasts, step up to the next model rather than running this one flat out.

Where this model fits

At 480 × 450 × 780 mm the CIM-006 is a floor-standing unit, not a countertop one — it needs a corner with a drain and a 380 W supply rather than a slot under a bar top.

The split-type construction means the condensing unit is separate from the ice-making head, which is the usual configuration where the machine sits in an enclosed back-of-house space and heat has to be rejected outside it.

Typical installations: cafés with an iced-drink menu, small restaurant bars, hotel pantry kitchens, and offices that run a beverage service through the day.

Questions

About the CIM-006

Is 80 kg/24h enough for a 60-seat restaurant?

Usually yes, with margin. A 60-cover restaurant running two services typically consumes 60–120 kg across the day depending on how beverage-led it is. The 80 kg/24h rating covers the lower half of that range; if the menu is drink-heavy or there is a summer terrace, look at the 100–120 kg models in the same series.

Can the CIM-006 be private labelled?

Yes. Logo, front panel colour, control wording, packaging and manual can all carry your brand, and voltage, plug type and refrigerant are configured for your destination market. MOQ for OEM orders starts lower than the 100–300 unit stock-model band — send the specification and we will confirm.

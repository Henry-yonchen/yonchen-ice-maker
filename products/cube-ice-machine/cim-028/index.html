CIM-028 Cube Ice Maker — 1,200 kg/24h

The largest machine in the range: 1.2 tonnes of cube ice a day into a 455 kg bin, two metres tall, split-type so the condensing unit can sit outside the ice room.

What 1,200 kg/24h is for

This is not a restaurant machine; it is a supply machine. At 1.2 tonnes a day the realistic buyers are central kitchens producing for several outlets, chain distribution centres, catering operations with peak weekends, and hotels where multiple outlets share one ice source.

The 455 kg bin is roughly nine hours of production — enough to cover a night when the machine is off, or to load a morning distribution run. If the operation needs more storage than that, the answer is a separate ice store, not a bigger machine.

Where it fits

2,000 mm tall, 1,230 × 960 mm footprint, 3,400 W. Split type means the condensing unit is separate from the ice-making head, so heat can be rejected outside the room where ice is stored — which is the point at this scale.

Installation is a project: dedicated power, structural consideration for the weight when the bin is full, drainage, and service access on both units.

Questions

About the CIM-028

Can several outlets share one CIM-028?

Yes, that is a common configuration — one machine and bin with insulated transport containers to the outlets. The constraint is logistics rather than production: how far and how often the ice travels.

What does split type mean in practice?

The condensing unit sits remotely, so the ice-making head does not dump heat into the room it stands in. On a 1.2 tonne machine that difference decides whether the ice room stays usable.

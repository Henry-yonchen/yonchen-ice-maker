What 3,000 kg/24h means in practice

Three tonnes a day is a plant-scale figure. For seafood processing it corresponds to chilling a substantial daily catch or keeping a large display and packing floor supplied through a shift. For concrete batching it is dosed into the mix in place of part of the mixing water, where the requirement follows the mix design and the target placement temperature rather than a fixed ratio.

The 600 kg bin is the working buffer between production and use. On a plant floor that is roughly a fifth of daily output — enough to absorb a dosing cycle or a truck loading, not enough to store a day's production. Plan storage or conveying capacity to match.

Where this model fits

At 1600 × 1380 × 2600 mm and 10,170 W this is installed equipment, not an appliance: it needs a dedicated power supply, plant-level drainage, and enough clearance for the condenser to be serviced. It is not a drop-in replacement for a commercial cube machine.

Typical installations: seafood processing and packing halls, port and fishery cold stores, concrete batching plants in hot climates, and chemical or pharmaceutical processes that use flake ice for temperature control.

Because output falls as ambient temperature rises, installations in unconditioned plant rooms should be sized above the nominal figure — plan on the hottest month, not the annual average.

Questions

About the FIM-007

Why specify 304 stainless steel on the evaporator?

The evaporator is the surface the ice forms on and the part that stays wet. In a food plant or a coastal installation, a 304 stainless evaporator resists corrosion and is cleanable in a way that a coated or lower-grade surface is not. It is also the first thing a food-safety audit will ask about.

Can this machine run on a site generator?

It can, but the supply has to be specified with the machine rather than assumed — starting current on the compressor is the constraint, not running load. Tell us the site supply at enquiry stage and we will confirm the configuration.

Why the bin is 70% of the daily output

A 140 kg bin on a 200 kg machine is a deliberate ratio. Flake ice is a working material, and working materials are consumed in batches: the bin absorbs a display dressing or a packing run, and the machine refills between them.

Two hundred kilograms a day suits a fish counter dressed twice a day, a small processing line, or a concrete trial batch — not a full plant. It is the model that lets a buyer test flake ice in their operation without committing to a three-tonne installation.

Where it fits

1,540 mm tall, 580 × 830 mm footprint, 1,500 W, 304 stainless evaporator. It needs a drain, condenser clearance, and a plan for moving ice from the bin to where it is used.

Typical installations: fish counters and small seafood shops, restaurant kitchens using flake for display, small concrete or chemical operations, and processors trialling flake before scaling.

Questions

About the FIM-001

Is 200 kg enough for a fish counter?

For a counter dressed twice a day, usually yes. The 140 kg bin is the operative number: if the counter needs more than 140 kg in a single dressing, this model will run continuously to keep up.

Why 304 stainless on the evaporator?

The evaporator stays wet and is the surface ice forms on. 304 resists corrosion and can be cleaned properly — in a food operation that is a specification requirement, not a premium.

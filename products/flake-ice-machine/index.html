What this series is for

Flake ice is thin, dry and mouldable — it packs around a fish without bruising it, and it absorbs heat faster than cube ice per unit of contact area. That is why seafood processors, fishing vessels and concrete plants specify flake rather than cube.

FIM machines run 200 to 3000 kg/24h with a 304 stainless steel evaporator and sub-cooled flake at −5 °C to −8 °C. Above 3000 kg/24h, projects are usually configured as multiple units or a custom build.

Typical applications

  • Seafood processing and display
  • Fishing vessel and port
  • Concrete cooling
  • Chemical and pharmaceutical processing

Buying Questions

Industrial Flake Ice Machines — questions buyers ask

Why is flake ice preferred for seafood?

Flake ice is soft and mouldable, so it covers the whole surface of the product without puncturing skin, and it chills faster than cube ice. That extends shelf life and protects appearance — which is what buyers are actually paying for.

Can flake ice machines run in a marine environment?

Marine and fishery installations need corrosion protection and a stable power supply. Specify the installation conditions when enquiring, because the configuration differs from a land-based plant.

How is capacity affected by ambient temperature?

Rated output is measured under standard conditions. In a hot plant the same machine produces less per day, so size up by roughly 15–20% when ambient temperature stays above 35 °C.

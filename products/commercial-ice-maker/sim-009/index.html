What 100 kg/24h and a 20 kg bin cover

A hundred kilograms a day is roughly 400–500 drinks, or 120–150 restaurant covers across two services. The 20 kg bin is the buffer: enough to absorb a lunch rush, not enough to carry a venue through a full day without the machine running.

Scheduled ice making is the practical feature — the bin is full before opening instead of the machine being heard through service. Adjustable thickness is the other setting operators actually use, because thinner cubes read better in some drinks and last longer in others.

Where it fits

At 765 mm tall this is a floor-standing cabinet, not a countertop unit, and it needs clearance for the door and for condenser air. The 405 mm width is why it sells: it slides into gaps where a wider modular system will not go.

Typical installations: small restaurants, coffee shops, bars with a food menu, hotel pantry kitchens and staff canteens.

Questions

About the SIM-009

Is 100 kg/24h enough for a 100-seat restaurant?

Usually, with the 20 kg bin as the buffer. A 100-cover restaurant typically consumes 50–100 kg across the day depending on how beverage-led it is. With a terrace or a summer peak, plan the next size up.

Can it be built for my market's voltage?

Yes — voltage, frequency and plug type are configured per destination market, and the refrigerant is matched to the regulation in force there. Confirm the market at enquiry stage.

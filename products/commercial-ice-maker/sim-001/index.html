SIM-001 Compact Commercial Cube Ice Maker — 25 kg/24h

The entry point into commercial cube ice: 25 kg a day in a countertop cabinet, for places that need real cube ice but have no room for a floor-standing machine.

Where 25 kg/24h stops being a home machine

The number is close to a large domestic unit; the difference is duty cycle and build. A commercial 25 kg machine is expected to run through a service, not through a household evening. That is why it appears in small bars, office pantries and convenience counters rather than in kitchens.

The 2.5 kg bin tells you the working pattern it is designed for: continuous service with frequent draws, not storage. A venue that must hold ice through a lunch and a dinner without producing in between needs a bigger bin, not more daily output.

Where it fits

360 × 440 × 450 mm sits on a counter or a low cabinet, and at 200 W it runs from a standard socket. Self-contained means the condenser is inside the cabinet — it needs air around it and will reject heat into the room.

Typical installations: small bars and cafés as a backup or garnish machine, office pantries, staff kitchens and convenience counters selling iced drinks.

Questions

About the SIM-001

Can SIM-001 replace a full commercial machine in a bar?

For garnish and low-volume service, yes. For a working bar well through a busy night it will not keep up — the bin holds 2.5 kg and recovery is capped by the 25 kg daily rating. Step up within the series for that.

Does it need a drain?

It needs somewhere for meltwater and cleaning water. A gravity drain or a container is normally enough at this size; confirm the installation conditions before quoting an end user.

What this series is for

SIM covers the gap between consumer countertop units and full commercial modular systems. Nine models run from 25 to 100 kg/24h with cube ice, stainless steel construction and air or water cooling depending on model.

This is the series most often bought by hospitality distributors: one SKU list serves cafés, bars, small hotel kitchens and convenience stores, and spare parts are shared across the range.

Buying Questions

Commercial Ice Makers — questions buyers ask

Air cooled or water cooled — which should I stock?

Air cooled is simpler to install and needs no water drain for the condenser; water cooled handles hot ambient kitchens better. Stock the type that matches local installation conditions, or carry both for larger distributors.

Can SIM machines run continuously through service hours?

Yes — these are commercial-duty machines with storage bins sized for peak service. For higher volume than 100 kg/24h, step up to the CIM series.

Do you supply spare parts for this series?

Yes. Spare parts, installation guidance and remote technical support are supplied with the machines, and SKD kits are available for local assembly.

What this series is for

Granular ice is softer and chews easily, which is why it drives drink sales in beverage chains and why supermarkets use it for seafood and produce display. GIM runs from 30 kg/24h compact units up to 650 kg/24h machines for continuous display duty.

Food-contact parts are 304 stainless steel. For a display counter, match the machine's daily output to the display bed size — under-sized machines run continuously and wear faster.

Typical applications

  • Bubble tea and beverage chain
  • Supermarket seafood display
  • Hotel breakfast and buffet
  • Food processing and cold chain

Buying Questions

Nugget & Granular Ice Machines — questions buyers ask

How much ice does a display counter need?

A practical rule for display beds is 1.5–2 times the visible ice volume per shift, because ice melts and is topped up. Choose the model whose 24h output covers your daily top-up requirement rather than the display volume alone.

Is nugget ice more expensive to run than cube ice?

Nugget machines use an auger and extruder, so maintenance points differ from cube machines, but running cost is driven mainly by ambient temperature and how often the bin is opened. Keep the condenser clean and the bin closed.

Can GIM machines be private labelled?

Yes — logo, panel colour, control interface, packaging and manual are all customisable, and voltage, plug type and refrigerant are configured for the destination market.

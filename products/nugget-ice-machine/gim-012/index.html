What 650 kg/24h changes

Six hundred and fifty kilograms is supermarket and processing territory. At 0.2–0.25 kg per drink it is far beyond beverage service; the realistic uses are seafood and meat display beds and food processing where ice is a working material.

The 200 kg bin is about a third of daily output — enough to absorb a display dressing cycle or a shift change, not enough to store a day. Plan storage or conveying alongside the machine, because a 650 kg machine feeding a 50 kg display is a machine running at part load.

Where it fits

1,850 mm tall, 760 × 850 mm footprint, 2,900 W. This needs a dedicated supply, plant-level drainage and service clearance on the condenser side. It is not a unit you wheel into a shop and plug in.

Typical installations: supermarket seafood and meat counters, seafood processing halls, central kitchens, and cold-chain operations needing a consistent granular supply.

Questions

About the GIM-012

Can this run continuously?

It is built for continuous duty, but output is rated under standard conditions. In an unconditioned plant room above 35 °C, plan on 15–20% less real output and size accordingly rather than running the machine flat out.

What maintenance does the auger need?

The auger and evaporator need scheduled descaling and the condenser needs regular cleaning — in a dusty plant that may mean weekly. Access is designed for it, but someone has to be assigned to the task.

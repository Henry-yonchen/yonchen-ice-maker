How the range is organised

Buyers usually arrive with one of three questions: how much ice per day, what shape of ice, and whether the machine is for a consumer or a professional installation. The series map follows those three questions.

  • Consumer channels — PIM (12–15 kg/24h, value line) and AIM (12–60 kg/24h, touch and AI voice control).
  • Light commercial — SIM (25–100 kg/24h, self-contained cube machines for cafés, bars and small kitchens).
  • Food service at scale — CIM (30–1,200 kg/24h, cube and crescent, 28 models).
  • Ice as a working material — GIM (30–650 kg/24h nugget and granular) and FIM (200–3,000 kg/24h sub-cooled flake).
Not sure which series fits your market? Send the daily ice volume, ambient temperature and target retail price — we will shortlist two or three models instead of quoting the whole catalogue.

What this series is for

PIM models are plug-and-play countertop machines for households, small offices, apartments and holiday rentals. All ten models sit in the 12–15 kg/24h band, so the range competes on form factor, ice shape and price point rather than output.

Because the bill of materials is stable, this series is the easiest to private-label: panel colour, logo and packaging change without touching the refrigeration system.

Typical applications

  • Home and apartment
  • Small office and studio
  • Holiday rental and Airbnb
  • Promotional and gift channel
All 10 models in this series can be private labelled: logo, panel colour, control interface, packaging, voltage, plug type and refrigerant are configured for your market. Samples ship in 7–10 days; bulk production 25–35 days.

Buying Questions

Portable Ice Makers — questions buyers ask

What is the difference between PIM and AIM models?

PIM is the value line: mechanical or simple electronic control, bullet and square cube ice. AIM adds touchscreens, colour displays and AI voice control, and extends to more ice shapes.

Do portable models need a water line?

No. These are manual-fill countertop machines — you pour water into the reservoir. That is what makes them usable without plumbing.

What is the typical first order for this series?

Standard models start around 100–300 units per model. Mixed-model containers are possible, and trial samples ship in 7–10 days.

What 15 kg/24h buys over 12 kg

The extra three kilograms a day is not the point. The 1.5 kg bin is: three times the storage of the entry model, which means the machine can build a buffer before guests arrive instead of producing to order. For a household with two or three ice-heavy moments a day, that buffer is the difference between always having ice and waiting for it.

The 120 W draw is genuinely lower than the smaller PIM-001's 165 W — worth knowing if you sell into markets where running cost is part of the pitch. Self-cleaning and the full-ice indicator are also the two features retail buyers ask about first.

Where it fits

230 × 290 × 300 mm sits comfortably on a kitchen counter or a bar top. Manual fill, no drain — same installation model as the rest of the portable range, which is why this series moves through appliance retail and e-commerce rather than through installers.

Typical buyers: homes replacing a fridge ice maker, small offices, holiday rentals, and gift or promotional programmes that need a modern specification without a commercial price.

Questions

About the PIM-010

Is the LCD panel customisable?

The panel artwork, logo and control wording can be changed as part of an OEM order. The touch interface stays the same across the range, which keeps the manual and the after-sales script consistent if you stock several models.

How long does the first batch take?

The machine produces in cycles rather than continuously and starts delivering usable ice within the first cycle after filling. Exact timing depends on water temperature and ambient conditions, so treat any published first-batch figure as indicative.

What can be customised

Customisation is split into two layers. The commercial layer — logo, colour, packaging, manual — is available on any model and needs no tooling. The technical layer — voltage, plug, refrigerant, control interface and ice shape — depends on the platform the model is built on.

  • Branding: logo, front panel colour and artwork, control panel wording, packaging design, user manual, rating label.
  • Electrical: 110–120 V or 220–240 V, 50/60 Hz, US / EU / UK / AU plug types, configured per destination market.
  • Refrigerant: matched to the regulation in force in your market at the time of order.
  • Control: mechanical, touchscreen, colour display or AI voice module, depending on series.
  • Logistics: SKD kits for local assembly, and mixed-model containers so one shipment can carry a full SKU range.

Minimum order quantity

MOQ depends on the model and whether the order is stock or OEM. Standard models start around 100–300 units per model; OEM/ODM orders can start lower depending on the specification. Trial samples are available before bulk orders, so the first step does not require a container commitment.

If you are launching a new brand, the usual path is: 2–3 sample models, one mixed container, then a repeat order with your own artwork locked in. That keeps the first order small without losing the OEM position.

Certification support

Machines are CE, ETL and ISO 9001 certified. For markets that need a national scheme, we arrange SASO, SAA, SABS, KC and PSE depending on destination. Tell us the destination country at enquiry stage — certification must be planned before production, not after.

After-sales and spare parts

Every machine ships with a warranty. We supply spare parts, installation guidance and remote technical support, and can provide SKD assembly kits if you want to assemble locally and reduce freight volume.

Process

From enquiry to container

Six steps, each with a defined output so nothing is assumed.

01

Enquiry

Send target capacity, ice type, market and estimated annual volume. We reply with 2–3 model options and FOB pricing.

02

Sample

Samples ship in 7–10 days. Test the ice, the panel and the packaging before committing to a tooling or artwork decision.

03

Specification lock

Voltage, frequency, plug type, refrigerant, control interface and packaging are fixed in writing before production.

04

Artwork and trial run

Logo, panel colour and manual are applied, then a small trial batch confirms the finish.

05

Bulk production

25–35 days for bulk orders depending on volume and customisation.

06

Inspection and shipping

Pre-shipment check, then FOB Ningbo or Shanghai; CIF, DDP and EXW also supported.

About YonChen — Ice Machine Manufacturer in Cixi, Ningbo

Ningbo Yongchen Shengzhi International Trade Co., Ltd. builds and exports ice machines from Cixi, the small-home-appliance manufacturing cluster in Zhejiang, China.

40,000sqm factory
100,000+units / year
60+export countries
76models in range

What we do

YonChen manufactures and exports ice machines for business buyers only: import distributors, wholesalers, restaurant and hotel chains, engineering procurement teams and private-label brand owners. We do not sell direct to consumers, which means our pricing and product decisions are built around container volumes rather than single-unit retail.

The range covers six series and 76 models from 12 kg/24h countertop machines to 3,000 kg/24h industrial flake ice units. Cube, crescent, bullet, nugget, granular, spherical, snowflake and flake ice are all produced on the same site.

Where we are

The factory is in Cixi, Ningbo, Zhejiang — one of China's densest small-appliance manufacturing clusters. Being inside the cluster matters for buyers in three practical ways: moulds and injection parts are sourced within a short drive, component suppliers are shared across product lines, and engineering changes move faster than for traders sourcing from scattered workshops.

Shipment is from Ningbo or Shanghai port, both within a few hours of the factory — see the factory page for the site, capacity and shipping details.

How we work with buyers

  • Sample first. Every new relationship starts with 1–3 sample models, shipped in 7–10 days.
  • Specification in writing. Voltage, plug, refrigerant, control interface and packaging are fixed before production.
  • Certification planned early. CE and ETL are standard; SASO, SAA, SABS, KC and PSE are arranged per destination country.
  • Support after shipment. Spare parts, installation guidance and remote technical support, plus SKD kits for local assembly.

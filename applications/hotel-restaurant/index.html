Ice Machines for Hotels and Restaurants

A hotel needs ice in several places at once: the bar, the banquet kitchen, room-service pantries and the buffet. The mistake is buying one large machine for the whole property instead of sizing each point of use.

Sizing by point of use

Bar and beverage service needs fast recovery and a bin sized for the busiest hour. Banquet and main kitchen needs volume across a long service. Pantries need a small, quiet, self-contained unit that housekeeping can operate without training. These are three different machines, not one.

For full-service restaurants a working estimate is 0.5–1 kg of ice per cover across a service, then 20% headroom for summer peaks. For banquet operations, add the ice used for display and buffet chilling, which is often the larger share.

Cube and crescent ice are the default for hotels because they melt slowly and look right in a glass. Nugget ice is a different decision — it is for chewing and for display, not for the bar well.

What to check before choosing

  • Count the points of use before choosing capacity — one machine per area usually beats one machine per property.
  • Self-contained (SIM) for pantries and small bars; modular or larger CIM units where volume is continuous.
  • Check door widths and service-lift dimensions: a 1,200 kg/day machine that cannot reach the kitchen is a liability.
  • Water quality drives maintenance load — a filter protects the evaporator and the warranty conversation.
Sizing is decided by the duty cycle, the ambient temperature and how the ice is used — not by floor area alone. Send the operating pattern and we will shortlist two or three models instead of quoting the whole range.

Questions

Common questions for this application

How many ice machines does a 200-room hotel need?

Typically four to six points: bar, main kitchen, banquet, and two or three pantry or floor-service locations. Exact count follows the layout, which is why we ask for a floor plan rather than a single capacity number.

Cube or crescent for a hotel bar?

Cube is the general default and the easier stock item. Crescent packs well in cups and is often specified by beverage-led outlets. Carrying both shapes in the 60–120 kg band covers most hotel needs.

Can machines be matched to an existing bin?

Modular units can be paired with bins, but the match has to be checked on dimensions and drop height. Send the bin specification with the enquiry.

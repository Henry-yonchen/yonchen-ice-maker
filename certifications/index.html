Standard on every production order

CE — European Union

Required to place the machine on the EU market. Covers electrical safety and, where applicable, electromagnetic compatibility.

ETL — North America

Third-party safety listing recognised for the US and Canadian markets, tested to the relevant UL/CSA standard.

ISO 9001 — Quality system

Process-level certification covering how production, inspection and corrective action are managed — not a product certificate.

Market-specific schemes

Beyond CE and ETL, some markets require a national certificate before customs clearance or retail listing. These are arranged per order:

  • SASO — Saudi Arabia
  • SAA — Australia
  • SABS — South Africa
  • KC — South Korea
  • PSE — Japan
Certification has to be planned at enquiry stage, not after production. Testing samples and documentation run in parallel with manufacturing, and a late request can delay a shipment by weeks.

What we send with a quotation

On request we provide certificate copies, the declaration of conformity, and the rating label artwork for your market — so your customs broker and your retail customer both have the paperwork they need.

A note on what certification does and does not mean

CE and ETL confirm that the electrical design has been tested to a recognised standard. They are not a warranty of product life, and they do not replace incoming inspection. Buyers running their own quality programme usually combine certificate checks with a pre-shipment inspection — we support both.

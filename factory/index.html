The Factory — 40,000 sqm in Cixi, Ningbo

Ice machine production from component sourcing to packing, on one site inside China's small-appliance manufacturing cluster.

40,000sqm site
100,000+units / year
6product series

What happens on site

Sheet metal, injection parts, assembly and packing are coordinated from the Cixi site. Six product series share the same production base, which is why a distributor can build a full SKU range from one supplier instead of assembling a catalogue from several factories.

Annual output runs above 100,000 units. Capacity is allocated by series, so a large cube-machine order does not block flake or nugget production.

Why the location matters

Cixi sits inside a cluster that supplies injection moulding, sheet metal, compressors, controllers and packaging within a short driving distance. For a buyer this shows up as three things: faster engineering changes, lower component inventory risk, and the ability to run small trial batches before committing to a full container.

Quality and compliance

Production follows the ISO 9001 quality system, and machines are CE and ETL certified. Market-specific schemes — SASO, SAA, SABS, KC, PSE — are arranged for the destination country before production starts, because test samples and documentation have to be prepared in parallel with manufacturing.

Visits can be arranged in Cixi. For buyers who cannot travel, we run a live video walkthrough of the line and the model you are considering.

Shipping

Goods ship FOB Ningbo or Shanghai, with CIF, DDP and EXW terms also supported. Both ports are within a few hours of the factory, so container booking does not add inland transit time.
